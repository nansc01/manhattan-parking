# Manhattan Free Parking

A phone web app that shows where you can park for free in Manhattan right now.

- Your location as a blue dot on a live street map
- Every block colored by NYC DOT's current parking signs: green = free now, orange = meter, red = no parking
- The closest free blocks, with how long they stay free and a Go button for driving directions
- Plan ahead with +1 / +2 / +4 hour views
- Knows the 2026 street-cleaning and meter holidays

Sign data: [NYC Open Data, Parking Regulation Locations and Signs](https://data.cityofnewyork.us/Transportation/Parking-Regulation-Locations-and-Signs/nfid-uabd), loaded live.

Always read the sign on the street before you park. Temporary signs (construction, film shoots, events) are not in the data.
